The X dig lane now enumerates neighborhoods, not just keywords: each round the seed miner emits `url:<page>` reverse searches (every post linking a found launch/repo/article, regardless of wording), `conversation_id:` pulls of the hottest threads' replies, and `@handle` sweeps of recurring accounts — turning every lucky hit into its surrounding cluster. `LAST30DAYS_X_DIG_NEIGHBOR` tunes seeds per round (default 3, 0 disables).
